I’ve learned a lot in the past two months.

I’ll start with what they don’t tell you: It’s important to take water breaks. The past few months have been both a delight and nonstop. You’ll never be ready, the list of todos is constantly growing. Cara and I are often dividing tasks to take care of Aspen (or Luna and Veela) just to get everything done. We’ve had such fun hosting family and friends, but some days are long.

Two months ago, Cara and I decided we’d put off setting up the car seat long enough. That turned out to be good timing, as Aspen decided she wanted to arrive a little earlier than her expected due date. We got to the hospital in the evening, and by morning we were holding our daughter. We spent another evening in the hospital post-delivery. They gave Aspen a few hats, and then they let us bring her home.

And suddenly, our lives are totally different.

Everything takes longer. Someone needs to constantly watch the kiddo, and getting ready in the morning (say, for taking Luna on a walk) takes a bit more coordination.

![Benjamin and Aspen][1]

We have lots of screaming. When Aspen first arrived, Luna didn’t know how to respond to the baby’s cries—so the pup would try to lick the little one for a few seconds, and failing to calm the baby, Luna herself would decide to join in by howling. (As a cat, Veela was not amused.) Thankfully, Luna soon learned that Aspen will cry, and it’s okay for the pup to just chill.

![Bill and Mary and Aspen][2]

The puppy loves her baby sister. Previously, Luna would follow Benjamin everywhere. Now, the pup follows whomever is carrying Aspen 🥹

![Cara and Aspen][3]

Two months goes fast. We’ve gone on lots of walks. Saw our families and friends quite a lot. Enjoyed a few mid-week wine tastings. But more important, we’ve been able to spend time with the little one. Watching her react to her puppy, her grandparents, and the world around her.

![Benjamin, Cara, Aspen, and Luna in Yosemite][4]
